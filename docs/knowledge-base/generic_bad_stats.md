# Generic Bad Stats & "Trap" Stats (Season 10)

## The "Noise" and Trap Stats
1. **Off-Element / Non-Synergistic Damage**: Damage types not explicitly used by your skills. If you're a Pyromancer, "Wind Damage" or "Physical Damage" rolls are completely useless. Always check skill tooltips (T) to see exactly what tags your skills scale with.
2. **Over-investing in Attack Speed**: Attack speed has diminishing returns. Stacking it exclusively will eventually provide significantly less value than flat damage, elemental damage, or survivability stats.
3. **Stat-Increasing Relics (Minor Stats)**: Relying on relics that only provide minor base stat increases is a trap. Experienced players focus on relics that provide **Weapon Effects** or large multiplicative damage bonuses.
4. **Splitting Stats**: A huge character-building pitfall. Do not spread your attribute points evenly. Once you have a comfortable amount of Vitality for survival, dump everything else into your primary damage stat (Strength or Intelligence).
5. **Neglecting Accuracy**: For physical/attack-based builds, missing attacks means dealing zero damage. Failing to cap or invest in sufficient accuracy is a hidden damage trap that makes high-level content feel impossible.
6. **Pure Damage on Mercenaries**: Trying to make your mercenary a DPS machine is a trap. They will die often to high-tier mechanics, and their revive costs will drain your gold. Build them purely for auras, survival, and Magic Find instead.
