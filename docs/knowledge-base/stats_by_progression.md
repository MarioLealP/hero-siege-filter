# Stats by Progression (Season 10)

## 1. Leveling Phase (Normal to Hell, Levels 1-100)
- **Primary Goal**: Complete acts and reach level 100 as efficiently as possible.
- **Stat Focus**:
  - **Vitality**: Extremely important early on. Put 60-100 points into Vitality to build a baseline health pool that prevents you from being one-shot by bosses or elite packs.
  - **Core Attribute**: Put the rest of your points into your main scaling stat (Strength for Physical/Attacks, Intelligence for Elemental/Spells).
  - **Base Damage**: Focus on skills with strong base damage scaling and upgrade weapons for high raw damage.
- **Defense**: Rely primarily on Vitality points and baseline Armor/Resistances found on leveling gear.

## 2. Mapping Phase (Early Endgame, Inferno)
- **Primary Goal**: Transition into the endgame (Incarnation Tree) and begin farming.
- **Stat Focus**:
  - **Capping Resistances**: The game applies severe resistance penalties in higher difficulties. Capping Fire, Cold, Lightning, and Poison is strictly mandatory.
  - **Stat Dumping**: Stop splitting stats. Transition into putting 100% of your available attribute points into your primary damage stat to maximize output.
  - **Synergies**: Start meticulously checking gear to ensure it specifically boosts your main element or skill tags. Use the town practice targets with the DPS tooltip enabled to verify upgrades.

## 3. Deep Endgame (Wormholes, Chaos Towers, Torment III+)
- **Primary Goal**: Maximizing clear speed and pushing high tiers safely.
- **Stat Focus**:
  - **100% Ignore Resistance / Defense**: Mandatory to actually deal damage to high-tier mobs with massive innate defenses.
  - **Defensive Layering**: Do not rely purely on a large HP pool. You need Damage Reduction %, Armor, and high Life. For melee classes, it is heavily advised to dedicate at least half of your gear affixes strictly to survival. Ranged casters can afford slightly more damage focus due to mobility.
  - **Efficiency Stats**: Movement Speed, reaching precise Attack Speed caps, and optimizing skill proc mechanics to speed up clears and handle dense packs.
