# Mercenaries in Season 10 (Ebontharn)

## How Mercenaries Work
In Season 10, Mercenaries are **NOT** designed to deal damage. Trying to build them for DPS is a major trap because they die easily in higher difficulties (Hell/Inferno), and their revive costs will quickly drain your gold reserves. 
Instead, they should be treated strictly as **Utility Supports (Auras)** and **Magic Find (MF) carriers**.

## Best Stats for Mercenaries
1. **Magic Find (MF)**: The absolute best stat for a mercenary. A portion of their MF passively carries over to your character, boosting overall loot drops. 
   - **Socketing**: Stacking **Ist Runes** (15% MF per rune) in your mercenary's socketed gear (helm, armor, shield, weapon) is the standard endgame play.
2. **Defensive Stats (All Res, Damage Reduction)**: Keeping your mercenary alive is priority number two. A dead mercenary provides no auras and no MF.
   - **Um Runes**: Use these in sockets for All Resistances if they are dying too frequently.
   - **Armor & Life**: Look for high base defensive stats to prevent them from getting one-shot by stray projectiles or traps.

## Best Mercenaries to Hire
1. **Knight (Act 1)**: The most popular and reliable choice for Season 10. They are naturally tanky and have a skill tree packed with defensive passives. Max out their damage reduction and armor skills to keep them alive, then pick up utility (stun/taunt).
2. **Archer (Act 3)**: Chosen primarily for attack speed buffs or to wield an **aura-providing weapon** (like *Valkyrie*). They have much lower survivability than the Knight, so they require heavy defensive gear investment to remain useful.
3. **Magister (Act 6)**: Generally considered the weakest option. They arrive late in the campaign and lack both the aura utility of the Archer and the raw tankiness of the Knight.

## General Strategy & Tips
- **Wait to Hire**: Do not hire or heavily invest in a mercenary early on. Wait until you actually have spare Magic Find gear and defensive socketables ready to equip them.
- **Skill Allocation**: Always prioritize their defensive passives in the skill tree to minimize revive costs and maximize uptime.
