# Generic Good Stats (Season 10)

## Top Priorities for All Builds
1. **Elemental Resistances**: This is the absolute minimum requirement. Resistances must be capped as you move into Torment III and beyond. Without capped resistances, you will get one-shot by basic elemental attacks.
2. **Main Stat**: The primary attribute that scales your main damage skill (Strength for physical/melee, Intelligence for casters).
3. **Ignore Enemy Resistance / Ignore Target Defense**: In endgame content (Wormholes), enemies have high defenses and resistances. You want to reach 100% Ignore Enemy Resistance for your chosen element, or Ignore Target Defense for physical attack builds.
4. **Damage Reduction & Armor**: Essential for all classes, but especially melee classes, to survive mistakes. Defensive layering (mixing a high Life pool, Armor, and DR%) is far better than stacking just one defensive stat.
5. **Weapon Base Damage & Attack Speed**: Your weapon is your biggest source of damage scaling. Always prioritize high base damage and proper attack speed/scaling modifiers for your specific build.

## Secondary Good Stats
1. **Critical Hit & Multipliers**: Once survivability and primary stat are sorted, focus on specialized damage multipliers (Crit, % Damage) to scale DPS.
2. **Movement Speed**: Crucial for clear speed, avoiding boss telegraphs, and general efficiency.
3. **Magic Find**: Highly useful for farming and progression, especially if stacked efficiently on gear or placed on mercenaries.
